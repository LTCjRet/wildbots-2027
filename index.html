Decision criteria

A pair must pass both time rules; among those that do, the one with the most expected district points wins.

  1. Maximize qualifying points. Expected points per event come from how many forecast attendees are stronger than 3329 on 2026 results. In 2026 the 45th qualifier had about 49–51 pre-DCMP points; 3329 earned 64.
  2. Rebuild time between events: at least 7 clear days. That rules out back-to-back weeks, which leave only 3–5 days.
  3. Time before Macon: at least 7 clear days. DCMP runs Apr 7–9, 2027. A Week 5 event ends Apr 3, leaving 3 days to repair and reload.

Drive time from Kingsland is the tiebreaker, not a filter.

Why this order

Albany goes first because it is the scarce piece. It is in both of the top rule-passing pairs, has the smallest capacity, and is 3329's shortest drive (3.1 h). Winning it in Round 1 leaves two good Round 2 partners, Gainesville or Dalton.

Dalton is the Round 1 backup because it pairs with the most. It works with Albany (18 days apart) or Gwinnett (12 days apart), so it still leaves a rule-passing Round 2 option if Albany goes to other teams.

Gainesville ranks third despite being in the best pair. Its only rule-passing partner is Albany. Landing Gainesville in Round 1 and then missing Albany in Round 2 would force a back-to-back week or a Week 5 event.

Douglasville scores more but costs Macon prep. Douglasville pairs add 3–6 points on paper, but it ends Apr 3, four days before DCMP. Any damage or rebuild would carry straight into the event that decides the season. The forecast is also least certain there, since no team has a history at the new venue.

The gap between Gainesville + Albany and Dalton + Albany is about 2 points. That is well inside the model's per-event error (about ±9 points), so schedule and travel matter as much as the point estimate.

Method and sources

Figures come from the 3329 planner in the FRC PCH 2027 Competitive Outlook workbook (download .ods · download .xlsx).

  • Expected points per event = 79.1 − 19.6 × ln(1 + k), where k is the number of forecast attendees with a higher 2026 blended strength than 3329 (fitted on all 148 team-events of 2026, R² 0.81). It gives 3329 about 66 points for its actual 2026 fields versus 64 earned.
  • Forecast attendance scores every team's possible event pairs on past venue loyalty, drive time and week spacing, then fills events to capacity. Backtested on 2026, it matched 60% of event picks.
  • Strength blends 2026 average district points per event (40%) and qualification win rate (25%); EPA can be added in the workbook.
  • Drive times are estimates from Kingsland, calibrated on 68 routes (typical error about 10 minutes).
